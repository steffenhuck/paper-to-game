HOW TO PLAY

WHO YOU ARE

The leader of the big centre-right party. The radical right is the small, loud party that wants every election to be about immigration. The pink balloon is its share of the vote.

WHAT YOU DO

  • Each day, three reporters ask a question. Tap the one you will answer.
  • If it's about immigration, pick how you answer: like them (say what the radical right says), your usual line (what your party said last time) or against them (take them on).
  • Anything else you answer with your party's line.

WHAT YOU SEE

After each answer the balloon grows or shrinks: that is what your campaign so far hands the radical right. The dashed ring is the 1-point line. Under it: held. Over it: not held. Tomorrow's headline is what voters read.

TWO CAMPAIGNS

A quiet year first, then the refugee crisis, when some days every question is about immigration. Six days each, about two minutes each.

Swen Hutter & Hanspeter Kriesi (2022). Politicising immigration in times of crisis. Journal of Ethnic and Migration Studies 48(2): 341–365. doi:10.1080/1369183X.2020.1853902. Game built from this paper; the paper is the authority where the two disagree.
