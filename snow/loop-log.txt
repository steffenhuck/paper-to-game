VERDICT (band: high)
  SNOW: On the same streets, a house on Battersea water lost someone about seven times as often as a house on Thames Ditton water in these seven weeks, and fourteen times as often in the first four. Crowded or genteel, by the river or on the hill: once the water was known, the parish made no difference.
  YOU: Behind the crape doors you tried, nearly all drank Battersea water; behind the living doors, a few. By your knocks, a house on Battersea water wore crape and no house on Thames Ditton water wore it at all, so the gap is wider than your knocks can count.
  PART: Your knocks found the gap even wider than his. Three streets are few; his were six hundred and fifty-eight deaths, and a death behind a blue door turns up about once in fifteen.
  HAD: Had the question been answered from the companies’ lists instead of at the door, as the Board of Health answered it, the gap would have shrunk to three and a half to one: with deaths six times as common behind one company’s doors, five mistakes in six move a death from the guilty company to the innocent one. Your knocks were made at the door.
  PARISH: Battersea, airy and well-to-do, had crape on seven doors; Lambeth Church, crowded and poor by the river, on one. The parish did not decide it. The pipe did.
  PREDICTION: On Borough Road, with the books open, crape came to five of the ten brown doors and one of the eight blue. You marked five brown and no blue; crape came to two of your five.
  RESIDENTS: Of the twenty-one doors you knocked at, three could not say whose water they drank, and of those who named one, two were wrong. Snow did not rest content with the reply: he asked for the water-rate receipt, and he carried the silver drops.
  LATER: As the epidemic went on the gap narrowed: fourteen to one in the first four weeks, eight to one in the next three, five to one in the last ten, as other ways of catching it, from hand to mouth in crowded rooms, joined the water. And it was not dirt in general: thousands drank from foul cisterns that summer unharmed. It was the specific matter of the disease in the specific water.
