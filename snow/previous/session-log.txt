street 4: A month has passed. Crape came to 6 doors: 5 of the 10 brown, 1 of the 8 blue. Of your five marks, 2 wear crape; 4 doors you did not mark do.
END: WHAT SNOW FOUND / On the same streets, a house on Battersea water lost someone about seven times as often as a house on Thames Ditton water in these seven weeks, and fourteen times as often in the first four. Crowded or genteel, by the river or on the hill: once the water was known, the parish made no difference. / WHAT YOU FOUND / Behind the crape doors you tried, nearly all drank Battersea water; behind the living doors, a few. By your knocks, a house on Battersea water wore crape and no house on Thames Ditton water wore it at all, so the gap is wider than your knocks can count. / WHERE YOU PART / Your knocks found the gap even wider than his. Three streets are few; his were six hundred and fifty-eight deaths, and a death behind a blue door turns up about once in fifteen. / HAD YOURS STOOD / Had the question been answered from the companies’ lists instead of at the door, as the Board of Health answered it, the gap would have shrunk to three and a half to one: with deaths six times as common behind one company’s doors, five mistakes in six move a death from the guilty company to the innocent one. Your knocks were made at the door. / YOUR WINDOW / BATTERSEA WATER / THAMES DITTON WATER / CRAPE / NO / CRAPE / SNOW’S WINDOW, FIRST SEVEN WEEKS, TWENTY-ONE SUBDISTRICTS / BATTERSEA WATER / THAMES DITTON WATER / CRAPE / NO / CRAPE / 507 / 98 / 15,531 / 20,456 / His top row is deaths in houses on each water; his bottom row is the other houses each company supplied, from the return made to Parliament. Five hundred and seven against ninety-eight, in sixteen thousand houses against twenty thousand. / THE PARISH AND THE PIPE / Battersea, airy and well-to-do, had crape on seven doors; Lambeth Church, crowded and poor by the river, on one.
