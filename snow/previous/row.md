| **Snow's Cloudy Glass** | Snow (1856), *Cholera and the Water Supply in the South Districts of London in 1854*, reprinted from the *Journal of Public Health*, October 1856. The grand experiment, as an 1854 London handbill: two companies' pipes run down the same streets, one drawing from the Thames inside the town and one from above it, and cholera has come. You are Snow at the door: three real streets from his tables, eighteen houses each with crape on the doors where someone died, seven knocks a street. Tap a door, hear what the resident thinks, draw a glass and add the silver drops: milky means the town's water, clear the upriver water; the door is painted and a house goes into a four-pane window, crape or no crape, brown or blue. Knock only where there is crape and the window's second row stays empty, which is what the Board of Health could not answer. Then Battersea, genteel and all on the town's water, against Lambeth Church, crowded and poor and nearly all off it; then Borough Road with the companies' books open, five doors to mark before a month passes. The verdict sets your window beside Snow's (507 against 98 deaths in 16,000 against 20,000 houses), in words. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/snow/snows-cloudy-glass.html) · [memo](snow/memo.md) |
