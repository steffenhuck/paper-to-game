# Snow's Cloudy Glass — note for the author (second build)

1. Look first at `screenshot-a-offer-1.png` (the doctor's free pair, with its card: two things different), `screenshot-a-3-result.png` (the Waterloo Road crape door against the door next to it, by the glass: one thing different), `screenshot-a-check-result.png` (a crape word checked and found wrong; the line under the window jumps), then `screenshot-a-end.png` and `screenshot-b-end.png` (the verdict for the walkthrough and for the doctor's way). Then play it once on a phone.
2. Everything tunable is in `CONFIG` at the top of `mechanism.js`, the only copy of the mechanism: the two street rows, the compression (10), the knocks (8) and the three costs, the residents' rates and the wrong-word floor, the doctor's offers, the crape floors, and the agreement band. Edit, run `node calibration.js` (55 checks), then `python3 build.py`, which runs the check again and refuses to build on a failure or a mismatched mechanism.
3. What we were least sure about: (a) the evidence in a morning is thin by design (one or two clean pairs, from Waterloo Road's crape door and Battersea's one blue house), so the line under the window speaks in words like "no blue door in these pairs wears crape" rather than a number near seven; (b) the doctor's marsh is our example of a district reading, not a quotation; (c) the floor of one wrong word at a Battersea crape door is what makes the drift meetable in every game, and About says so; (d) the "how" screen fills the phone to the last pixel at 380 × 760, and the result panel needs a scroll to read the statement under the glasses; (e) the offer panels are long and sit below the fold.

Second build, 15 November 2026, after the author's brief on the first: the game is now the two decisions of the paper (what to compare, how to know) rather than twenty-one knocks. The first build is in `previous/`.
