# Snow's Cloudy Glass — note for the author (fourth build)

1. Look first at `screenshot-a-map.png` (the map before any glass), `screenshot-a-2-result.png` (two glasses in: both hollow in the window, the doctor's objection), `screenshot-a-conceded.png` (the glass that makes him concede: the window's houses turn solid, the count is fixed), `screenshot-a-end.png` (the verdict: the headline on identification first, then the sentence judged against the tests, then the count against the benchmarks), and `screenshot-b-end.png`. Then play it once on a laptop: the bill widens to 600 px there and the type is 18 px.
2. Everything tunable is in `CONFIG` at the top of `mechanism.js`, the only copy of the mechanism: the six street rows, the two rates, the compression (10), the cap (10), the crape floors, the concession threshold, and the two benchmarks the verdict quotes (taken from the strategy table in the memo, section 18). Edit, run `node calibration.js` (44 checks), then `python3 build.py`, which runs the check again and refuses to build on a failure or a mismatched mechanism.
3. What we were least sure about: (a) the concession rule (one street, a tested brown house with crape and a tested blue house, brown worse by the paper's gap or no blue crape at all) is a yes/no on evidence that can be one pair, which is the idea but thin as proof, and the verdict says Snow had six hundred; (b) the benchmarks (second or third glass; nine or more) are from our strategy table and are quoted as plain sentences on the verdict; (c) the doctor's "inland air" and the river-to-inland order of the streets are ours; (d) on a phone the map, the window, the two lines and the drops button fill 760 px to the edge when the doctor's line runs to three lines.
