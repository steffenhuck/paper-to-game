# Snow's Cloudy Glass — note for the author (third build)

1. Look first at `screenshot-a-map.png` (the map before any test: crape inland, the doctor's line), `screenshot-a-3-result.png` (three tests in, the doctor's objection), `screenshot-a-5-result.png` (a brown crape house and the blue house beside it on one street; the window's first houses; the doctor's concession), then `screenshot-a-end.png` and `screenshot-b-end.png` (the verdicts for the walkthrough and for the doctor's way, with the map revealed). Then play it once on a phone.
2. Everything tunable is in `CONFIG` at the top of `mechanism.js`, the only copy of the mechanism: the six street rows (houses supplied by each company, Tables I and II), the two rates (Table I totals), the compression (10), the tests (8), the crape floors, and the agreement band. Edit, run `node calibration.js` (38 checks), then `python3 build.py`, which runs the check again and refuses to build on a failure or a mismatched mechanism.
3. What we were least sure about: (a) only a street where both waters were tested *and* a crape door was among them counts as answering the doctor, so an unlucky eight (a brown crape house whose neighbours are also brown, and no crape where the pipes mixed) ends "unsupported", which the verdict explains but a teenager may feel as unfair; (b) the doctor's "inland air" and the river-to-inland order of the six streets are ours; (c) the same death rate is used for every street (the paper says the relative mortality is nearly the same throughout, but the absolute rates differ by subdistrict); (d) the map's row tallies are small text at phone width.

Third build, 15 November 2026, after the author's brief on the second: one tool, one decision (where), the doctor as the confound. The second build is in `previous/`, the first in `previous/first/`.
