That is identification: the one thing that differs between the two houses is the one thing that can explain the difference between them. / YOUR SENTENCE / You wrote: “It is the Southwark and Vauxhall Company’s water.” Right, and your tests carry it. / HOW MANY GLASSES / The doctor conceded at your fifth glass, and you used six in all. The quickest plan, a crape door in a street where the pipes are mixed and then its neighbours until one comes up clear, gets there at the second or third glass in most games. Testing where the crape is takes nine or more, when it gets there at all. / WHAT SNOW FOUND / Snow compared houses on the same streets, where the only thing different was the pipe. The town’s water killed about seven times as often as the upriver water in these seven weeks, and fourteen times as often in the first four. And once the water was known, whether a street was by the river or inland, crowded or genteel, made no difference at all. / HAD YOURS STOOD / Had the question been answered from lists instead of at the door, as the Board of Health answered it, the gap would have shrunk to three and a half to one, because with deaths six times as common behind one company’s doors, five mistakes in six move a death from the guilty water to the innocent one.
