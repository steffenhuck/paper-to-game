| **Snow's Cloudy Glass** | Snow (1856), *Cholera and the Water Supply in the South Districts of London in 1854*, reprinted from the *Journal of Public Health*, October 1856. The grand experiment as a lesson in spatial identification, on an 1854 London handbill: a map of six of Snow's subdistricts from the river inland, six houses each, pipes mixed house by house in each street's real proportion, and crape already on the doors, mostly inland. The parish doctor says the inland air. You have a glass, the silver drops and eight tests, anywhere: tap a house, watch the glass turn milky or stay clear, and the door is painted; every street keeps a tally and the doctor answers each test as far as your evidence lets him. Test inland and your brown houses are inland, like the crape; test one street where both pipes run and the crape is at the brown doors with the same air at both. One sentence for the Board, then the verdict reads it against your own tests and shows the map with every pipe revealed.
