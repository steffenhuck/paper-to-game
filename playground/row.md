| **Me and My Friends** | Bagues & Zinovyeva (2025), *Gender Segregation in Childhood Friendships and the Gender-Equality Paradox*, working paper, University of Warwick. Half a million children in 37 countries were asked how many of their close friends are boys and how many are girls; the richer, more gender-equal countries are where they mix least, at eleven, before anyone picks a subject. As a child's crayon drawing on sugar paper: build a country from three cards (the houses, who gets the jobs, what parents say a child should learn), ring the school bell, and watch ten kids draw who they are friends with, boys on one side and girls on the other, a red line for a friend from across the middle. Build the country you would want to live in and it comes out at the Finland end of the ruler. Then look inside one British street, five small houses and five big, and try to get the big-house kids mixing like the small-house kids with the mums' views, what a child should learn, and a week of clubs or the street: the mums' lever runs the other way from the map, and the gap will not close. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/playground/me-and-my-friends.html) · [memo](playground/memo.md) |
