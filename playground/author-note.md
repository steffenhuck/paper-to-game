# Me and My Friends — note for the authors

A gift for Manuel Bagues and Natalia Zinovyeva, built from the July 2025 working paper.

1. Look first at `screenshot-a-1-result.png` (the rich, equal, think-for-yourself country, rung: six of ten, the Finland end), then `screenshot-a-reveal.png` (three built countries on the ruler beside Finland and Portugal), `screenshot-b-1-result.png` (inside one street: the mums' lever goes the other way) and `screenshot-a-short.png` (why the gap won't close). Then play it once on a phone.
2. Everything tunable is in `CONFIG` at the top of `mechanism.js`, the only copy of the mechanism: the Figure 3 correlations, Finland and Portugal, the Figure 4 quintiles (the middle three read off the bars), the age-11 marginal effects from Tables 4, 5 and A14, your own decomposition shares (an eighth, a quarter), and everything ours (what a big house typically does, the 1.5 SD lever ends, the clubs floor, ten kids and twelve lines). Edit, run `node calibration.js` (42 checks), then `python3 build.py`, which refuses to build if the embedded mechanism differs from the file.
3. What we were least sure about: (a) the country dials are levers only in the game; each is weighted by its own bivariate correlation, and where the equality dial moves without the parents' dial the game prints your own hedge rather than a number it trusts; (b) the "typical big house" offsets (norms +0.2 SD, clubs +0.4, roaming −0.4, half of them permissive) are ours, and the shortfall survives with them set to zero; (c) pulling the levers to ±1.5 SD closes about half the Figure 4 gap in the toy, against the eighth-plus-quarter you measure from what families actually do, and the shortfall text says that is the difference between could and do; (d) the three equality measures are one dial, drawn as who gets the jobs; (e) the verdict reads the Table 7 chain to sixteen in words ("slightly less boyish or girlish subjects"): 0.067 SD is small, and the game says so.

This game has had the builder's review only: screenshots and a scripted browser session at 380 px. It has not been played by real strangers, and its length (five to seven minutes) is an estimate.
