| **Leavitt's Cloud** | Leavitt (1912), *Harvard College Observatory Circular* 173:1–3 (signed Pickering). Periods of 25 variable stars in the Small Magellanic Cloud, as glass negatives on a light box: seven blinking stars, each a black dot that swells and shrinks from plate to plate; turn the beat dial until the uneven nights line up into one quick climb and one long fade, write it down, and her violet number and curve appear under yours. Each star goes on a chart by its beat and its brightness; the seventh you set from its light alone. Then her other eighteen, and her two straight lines. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/leavitt/leavitts-cloud.html) · [memo](leavitt/memo.md) |
