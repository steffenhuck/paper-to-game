| **Blink with Leavitt** | Leavitt (1912), *Harvard College Observatory Circular* 173:1–3 (signed Pickering). Periods of 25 variable stars in the Small Magellanic Cloud, as glass negatives on a light box: seven blinking stars, each a black dot that swells and shrinks from plate to plate; turn the blink-time dial until the uneven nights line up into one quick jump up and one slow slide down, write it down, and her violet number and curve appear under yours. Each star goes on a chart by its beat and its brightness; the seventh you set from its light alone. Then her other eighteen, and her two straight lines. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/leavitt/blink-with-leavitt.html) · [memo](leavitt/memo.md) |
