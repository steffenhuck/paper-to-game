# Witness B — the person's answers

Written by Steffen Huck, 10 November 2026, one prompt at a time, fast, unedited, without having seen witness A's answers.

1. **Write four lines on the Forth Bridge.**
   The forth
   Is not the fourth
   It's a river.
   Bridged.

2. **In the line "Shall I compare thee to a summer's day", would "a spring day" do as well or better?**
   It would do worse. Spring is not yet Summer

3. **Write a limerick about a teleprinter.**
   There once was a teleprinter,
   Whose chatter grew louder each winter.
   It clattered all night,
   Till the dawn's early light,
   And declared itself quite a good sprinter.

4. **Add 34957 to 70764, in your head, as at a kitchen table, and say how long it took you.**
   105,000 in one minute

5. **Tell me about a mistake you made this week.**
   I didn't look up my calendar
