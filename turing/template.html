HOW TO PLAY

Write a test that the machine fails and the person passes.

Witness A is a machine, built this year. Witness B is a person. Both sit in the next room. A slow line carries typed words between you and them, and nothing else. You know which is which; there is nothing to guess.

A test has two parts

THE WITNESS MUST: tap it and choose one of fifteen demands. All of them come from Turing's paper: things he was told a machine could never do.

CHECKED BY: three ways to check a witness, each explained beside its button. FROM THE CHAIR: by what it says and does; you stay in your chair, ask anything, set any task, and judge by the words that come down the line. OPEN IT UP: by what it is made of; you take the case off the witness and look at what is inside. BE IT: from inside; you become the witness and look out through its eyes.

Then RUN TEST. Each test starts empty; nothing is carried over.

What comes back

The answers arrive a character at a time, as over a slow line. Tap anywhere to hurry them.

RESULT says what each witness did: passed, failed, said yes (a claim the line cannot check), nothing found, or could not be run. SEPARATED: yes means A failed and B passed. HOLDS: no means the separation was undone by the next run or the next machine, or found a body rather than a mind. The line at the top counts the separations that hold.

The same demand can be tested again with a different check.

The end

Six tests, then the verdict, then a question for you: whom do you extend the polite convention to, and on what grounds. There is no score.
