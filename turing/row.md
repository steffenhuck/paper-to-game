| **Turing's Witnesses** | Turing (1950), *Mind* 59(236):433–460. Computing machinery and intelligence, played as the experiment Turing proposed: interrogators judge, and the statistics are the machine's score against his 1950 bar. On a 1970s terminal, green on black, a person (Steffen Huck) and a machine of the kind that built the game sit in the next room; both answered eleven questions from the paper, fast and unedited, the machine as itself, never having seen the person's lines, under a word limit equal to the person's length. Read both answers, say which the person wrote, be told. Right seven times or fewer in eleven, and the machine passes the test Turing proposed. Then his reply to the argument from consciousness, and the polite convention handed to you.
