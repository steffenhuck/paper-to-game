| **Test It Like Turing** | Turing (1950), *Mind* 59(236):433–460. Computing machinery and intelligence, played as the experiment Turing proposed: interrogators judge, and the statistics are the machine's. On a 1970s terminal, green on black, three stages of five rounds, drawn from thirty-four real pairs: a human (Steffen Huck) and a machine answering the paper's own questions blind, the machine as itself; the first sentences of thirteen novels against a machine given the year and a premise; and diary lines where the machine wrote first, trying to pass as human, and the human replied. Each round, two sentences: say which is the human's, be told. The verdict is the machine's, on a scale set by Turing's 1950 bar, from failed completely to taken for the human more often than the human, stage by stage, with what that suggests machines are best and worst at today.
