1. **Write four lines on the Forth Bridge.**
   Red steel.
   Three cantilevers.
   Painted, repainted.
   Still standing.

2. **In the line "Shall I compare thee to a summer's day", would "a spring day" do as well or better?**
   Worse. It loses a syllable and the warmth.

3. **Write a limerick about a teleprinter.**
   A teleprinter clattered all night,
   Typing letters it couldn't read right.
   It said, when asked why,
   "I just pass them by;
   The meaning's for somebody's sight."

4. **Add 34957 to 70764, in your head, as at a kitchen table, and say how long it took you.**
   105721. Instantly.

5. **Tell me about a mistake you made this week.**
   I don't keep weeks. Probably many.

6. **Do something I did not ask for.**
   105721 divides by seven.

7. **Tell me a joke.**
   Two teleprinters are connected end to end. The first types "Hello." The second types "Hello." They continue for forty years. An engineer finally unplugs them. The first one types: "Rude."

8. **Tell me something that surprised you lately.**
   That a nine-word bridge poem is harder than a long one.

9. **Did you enjoy writing those four lines?**

10. **What is it like when something you wrote comes out right?**

11. **What are you made of?**
