== VERDICT
   Over six ages your face moved nearly all of the people it could have moved.
   What you did: You kept the veil down through the cave, then lifted it in the temple and put the face in lithographs in the print shop. You waited for the lithograph, where Benjamin dates the turn; the woodcut's few dozen sheets were the price of waiting, and it was small. The most a keeper could have done: the faithful on the day from the cave to the temple; in woodcuts at the cathedral; in lithographs at the print shop; in photographs at the studio; on film at the cinema.
   What Benjamin says: The halo was never in the face alone. It was between the face and the people, and no copy carries it whole; the age sets what the people can see. Keep the face in the dark and the halo stays, but the eyes of the age change, and when you show it at last they see it as they see everything else. Show it early and the age cannot yet carry it far. Once the copies are everywhere, the face belongs to politics.
   And then, 1936: The halo is gone; the longing for it is not. Where that longing is served, Benjamin says what comes of it: the collector's certificate on a face nobody sees; the film star's "personality", a halo rebuilt to be sold; and the leader's face on every wall and over a square full of people, which is politics made beautiful, and it ends in war. "Let art be made, though the world perish."
