== VERDICT
   Your face held the people for nearly all of what was possible.
   What you did: You kept the veil down through the cave and the temple, then lifted it in the cathedral and put the face in print in the print shop. You opened where the halo was already thin and the age could carry the face far: about where Benjamin dates the turn.
   What Benjamin says: The halo was never in the face. It was the distance between the face and the people, and the age sets the distance. Hide the face and the halo still goes, because the people's eyes have changed; show it early and the age cannot yet carry it far. Once the copies are everywhere, the face belongs to politics.
   What that means: The halo is back, enormous, and the square is full. The people are given the face to look at instead of anything to own. Benjamin: this is politics made beautiful, and it ends in war. "Let art be made, though the world perish."
