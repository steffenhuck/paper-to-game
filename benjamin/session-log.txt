== VERDICT: OVER SIX AGES YOUR FACE MOVED ALL OF THE PEOPLE IT COULD HAVE MOVED.
   You kept the veil down through the cave, then lifted it in the temple and put the face in print in the cathedral. You put the face into the people's hands as soon as there was a way to print it, and followed each new way as it came. The most a keeper could have done: in the dark from the cave to the temple; in woodcuts at the cathedral; in lithographs at the print shop; in photographs at the studio; on film at the cinema.
   The halo was never in the face. It was the distance between the face and the people, and the age sets the distance. Hide the face and the halo still goes, because the people's eyes have changed; show it early and the age cannot yet carry it far. Once the copies are everywhere, the face belongs to politics.
   The halo is back, enormous, printed in red and black on every wall, and the square is full. The people are given the face to look at instead of anything to own. Benjamin: this is politics made beautiful, and it ends in war. "Let art be made, though the world perish."
   Under this game's assumptions, that is. A second run is a different plan: open earlier, open later, or never.
