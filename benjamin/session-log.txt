== VERDICT: YOUR FACE HELD THE PEOPLE FOR NEARLY ALL OF WHAT WAS POSSIBLE.
   You kept the veil down through the cave, the temple and the cathedral, then lifted it in the court and put the face in print in the print shop. You opened where the halo was already thin and the age could carry the face far: about where Benjamin dates the turn.
   The halo was never in the face. It was the distance between the face and the people, and the age sets the distance. Hide the face and the halo still goes, because the people's eyes have changed; show it early and the age cannot yet carry it far. Once the copies are everywhere, the face belongs to politics.
   The halo is back, enormous, and the square is full. The people are given the face to look at instead of anything to own. Benjamin: this is politics made beautiful, and it ends in war. "Let art be made, though the world perish."
   The best path, under this game's assumptions, keeps the veil down and first prints the face at the print shop, then puts it on the screen as soon as there is one. A second run is a different plan: open earlier, open later, or never.
