== VERDICT: 100/100 · OVER SIX AGES YOUR FACE MOVED ALL OF THE PEOPLE IT COULD HAVE MOVED. 
   You kept the veil down through the cave, then lifted it in the temple and put the face in woodcuts in the cathedral. You put the face into the people's hands as soon as there was a way to print it, and followed each new way as it came. The most a keeper could have done: the faithful on the day from the cave to the temple; in woodcuts at the cathedral; in lithographs at the print shop; in photographs at the studio; on film at the cinema.
   The halo was never in the face alone. It was between the face and the people, and no copy carries it whole; the age sets what the people can see. Keep the face in the dark and the halo stays, but the eyes of the age change, and when you show it at last they see it as they see everything else. Show it early and the age cannot yet carry it far. Once the copies are everywhere, the face belongs to politics.
   The halo is gone; the longing for it is not. Where that longing is served, Benjamin says what comes of it: the collector's certificate on a face nobody sees; the film star's "personality", a halo rebuilt to be sold; and the leader's face on every wall and over a square full of people, which is politics made beautiful, and it ends in war. "Let art be made, though the world perish."
   Under this game's assumptions, that is. A second run is a different plan: open earlier, open later, or never.
