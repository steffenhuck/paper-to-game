# Benjamin's Veil — note for the author

1. Look first at `screenshot-age5-veil-set.png` (the print shop: the curtains parted to "in print", the lithographed face, the halo thin before anything was done) and `screenshot-age6-passed.png` (the studio: the halftone face, the crowd in, the halo gone, and the counterfactual row showing it gone at every veil). Then `screenshot-fork-rally-war.png` and `screenshot-end-veiled.png` (the never-lifted run's verdict: the halo went anyway).
2. Everything tunable is in the `CONFIG` block at the top of `mechanism.js`, the only copy of the mechanism: the five veils and their nearness, the seven ages with their eyes multiplier, demand, reach table and ceiling (each tagged with the section of the essay it was read from), the price of the crowd against the halo (`W_CROWD`), the demand cut, and the four 1936 places. Edit, run `node calibration.js` (31 checks against the essay's claims and the game's framing, including the heuristics table and the robustness runs), then `python3 build.py`, which refuses to finish if the embedded mechanism differs from the file.
3. What we were least sure about: (a) the whole thing is a toy; the essay has no numbers, so every number is ours, and the About screen says so three times. The thing to argue with is the eyes schedule (1, 1, .95, .85, .7, .4, .2): it puts the big drops at lithography and photography, following sections I and VI, and it is what makes hiding fail. (b) The price: hold is halo plus reach at equal weight; the robustness runs show the optimum stays between the court and the studio at half and double price, but at double price "always the widest veil" gets within a tenth of the best, so the lesson "show early and the age cannot carry it" is weaker if the crowd is priced high. (c) The fork is the essay's politics put before a keeper as a choice; it is not scored, and the rally's "WAR" overlay is the epilogue in one word. Whether that lands or lectures is for a stranger to say.

This game has had the builder's review only: screenshots and a scripted browser session at 380 px (two runs: a sensible path ending at the rally, and the never-lifted path ending at the newsreel). It has not been played by real strangers, and the five-minute length is an estimate.
