# Benjamin's Veil — note for the author

1. Look first at `screenshot-age5-veil-set.png` (the print shop: the curtains parted to "in print", the lithographed face, the halo thin before anything was done) and `screenshot-age6-passed.png` (the studio: the halftone face, the crowd in, the halo gone, and the counterfactual row showing it gone at every veil). 2. Everything tunable is in the `CONFIG` block at the top of `mechanism.js`, the only copy of the mechanism: the seven rungs (in the dark, the day, coins, woodcuts, lithographs, photographs, film) with their halo cost and reach, the six ages with their eyes multiplier, demand and the rung they open (each tagged with the section of the essay it was read from), the three numbers of the tally (people doubling, counted by their square root, and a seventh for being seen), the demand cut, and the epilogue's text. Edit, run `node calibration.js` (40 checks against the essay's claims and the game's framing, including the heuristics table and five robustness variants), then `python3 build.py`.
3. What we were least sure about: (a) the whole thing is a toy; the essay has no numbers, so every number is ours, and the About screen says so. The things to argue with are the eyes schedule (1, 1, .9, .7, .45, .2), a multiplier on what a halo does to the people (never on the halo itself), which is what makes hiding fail; the carry of each means (presence 1, coin .3, woodcut .5, lithograph .6, photograph .7, film .35), which is what makes the splash on film fail; and the coin's price (it spends more halo than the woodcut for fewer eyes, and carries less: the tempting mistake at the temple). (b) The tally: people moved, with the late ages weighing more by a square root; the robustness runs in the calibration show the shape survives the obvious alternatives. (c) 1936 is read, not played: the fork of the first version was a reading dressed as a choice and was cut.

Revised twice after play (memo, section 19): the picture now carries over between ages, copies accumulate, the marble is warm, the cause of the thinning is said once, and the announcements name places only; then the age boundary made continuous (the previous age's last frame first, then the halo thins and the face re-renders).

This game has had the builder's review and two plays only: screenshots and a scripted browser session at 380 px (two runs: a sensible path ending at the rally, and the never-lifted path ending at the newsreel). It has not been played by real strangers, and the five-minute length is an estimate.
