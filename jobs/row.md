| **One Number, Please** | Angelovski, Brandts & Güth (2026), *Theory and Decision*, doi:10.1007/s11238-026-10132-9. Bidding for better jobs: gender differences in competitiveness without a real-effort task. A journalist wants one number; you run the lab, on a Windows 2000 terminal. Eight sessions of 24 students: tick what competing means on their screen (a race at sums, or a price for the job) and what they can win (one winner, a flat ladder, a steep ladder), run it, and read the two bars, men and women, into a notebook. Then write back one sentence, and the notebook is read against it. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/jobs/one-number-please.html) · [memo](jobs/memo.md) |
