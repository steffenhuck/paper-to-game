# Leibniz's Even Hand — note for the author

1. Look first at `screenshot-a-1-placards.png` (the ban-heavy page placed by all six, with the three sums under the bench), `screenshot-a-2-placards.png` (six grey question marks: the sign), `screenshot-a-1-selected.png` (a line selected on the page, the four tools) and `screenshot-a-end.png` (the verdict with "bending over backwards"). Then play it on a phone: the thing to judge is whether one reading of the cars quarrel is enough to learn the readers for the next two, and whether the third (four reasons against two) is hard in a good way.
2. Everything tunable is in `CONFIG` at the top of `mechanism.js`, the only copy of the mechanism: the lines a full, brief and reply take; the three readers' rules and their wording; the three quarrels (question, side names, stance labels, budget and minimum, every reason in full and brief and every reply); the words the game says. Change anything and run `node calibration.js`: it re-enumerates every page of every quarrel and tells you whether a fair page still exists and which ways of playing are caught. Then `python3 build.py`, which runs the check again and refuses to build on a failure or a mechanism mismatch.
3. What we were least sure about: (a) the gap-finder nets, so a left-out reason of one side can be squared by leaving one of that side's reasons unanswered; it is stated on screen as "unanswered minus left out", but a stricter reader (both counts must match) is a one-line change in `leans()` and would cut the fair pages by about a third; (b) the readers have no tolerance: one line places you, which is "picking out words everywhere" but may feel harsh; a tolerance is a threshold in `placedBy()`; (c) two reasons a side with no answers at all passes the short quarrel, because it is mirrored; Leibniz would call that ploy 4 twice over; (d) the translation's page address, `leibniz-translations.com/controversies.htm`, could not be checked from the build session (the site was unreachable); the credit lines and About use it, so check the link before sending the game on; (e) the quarrels' reasons are ours, written to be fair to both sides; the author of a side will disagree about its best reason.

This game has had the builder's review only: screenshots and scripted browser sessions at 380 px. It has not been played by real strangers, and its length has not been measured.
