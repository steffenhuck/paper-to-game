| **Throw It Past Noether** | Noether (1918), *Nachrichten von der Gesellschaft der Wissenschaften zu Göttingen*, Math.-phys. Klasse, 235–257. Invariant variational problems as a deck of nine changes, each with its formula on screen: say whether the change matters to the formula, then read the reason off the formula. Every change that doesn't wins a prize (energy, momentum, spin); red badges for the paper, pink for the traps. The ball's formula cares how fast the clock runs, so a fake throw breaks its energy law; the slide's formula doesn't, so nothing breaks its energy, which is no law at all: Hilbert's claim about energy in general relativity, proved by the second theorem. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/noether/throw-it-past-noether.html) · [memo](noether/memo.md) |
