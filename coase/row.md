| **Coase's Cows** | Coase (1960), *Journal of Law and Economics* 3:1–44. The problem of social cost, as a Dick Bruna picture book: you are the court, and six quarrels come to the bench with the paper's own arithmetic, a farmer and a rancher whose cows eat the corn, the strip the cows cross to drink, a lawyer who charges for any deal, a chimney over a hundred houses, Coase's railway whose sparks burn the fields, and the same railway with other figures. Three rulings each time, the paper's own (the harmer may carry on, must pay, or is banned); touch one and it stands on the signpost; rule, and watch the neighbours act on it, cows crossing, corn planted or given up, coins passing. Where the neighbours can talk, the ruling changes who pays whom and nothing else; where they cannot, it decides what the valley is worth, and making the railway pay stops the trains. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/coase/coases-cows.html) · [memo](coase/memo.md) |
