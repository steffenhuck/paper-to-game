# Coase's Cows — note for the author

1. Look first at `screenshot-q1-tally.png` (the first quarrel's tally: the purses, the bar, and the two ghost rulings, which is where the Coase theorem lands) and `screenshot-q5-tally.png` (the railway under liability: no trains, $210 of $230, and the line that one train would be best and no ruling gets it). Then play it once ruling "make them pay" every time, which is most strangers' first run: it loses only on the lawyer and the railway, and the verdict says why.
2. Everything tunable is in `mechanism.js`, the only copy of the mechanism: `CONFIG` at the top carries every number with its page in Coase or its reason (the meat values, the corn's worth, the lawyer's fee, the turnips, the chimney's and the orchard's figures are ours; the rest is Coase's); the six quarrels with their facts, rulings, signs and the preview lines are in `quarrels()`; every sentence the game says during play is in `beats()`, `plantLine()`, `countLineLiable()` and `resultLines()`; the verdict's three lines and the fates are in `verdict()`. Edit, run `node calibration.js` (120 checks against the paper), then `python3 build.py`, which refuses to finish if the embedded mechanism differs from the file. The drawings are in `template.html` (`buildFarm`, `buildSmoke`, `buildRail`, `icon`).
3. What we were least sure about: (a) the third steer brings $1, not $2, so that every bargain settles in whole dollars on screen; Coase's text allows either, and the check confirms the discoveries hold with $2 too (half dollars aside); (b) the chimney's and the orchard's figures are entirely ours, built on the setting Coase gives and no numbers; (c) the fate uses each quarrel's share of the possible equally, not the dollar sum, so that the railway's hundreds do not swamp the farm; the headline still gives the dollar sum; (d) under "the harmer must pay" the sufferer plants whatever is worth most standing, since he is made whole, which is what makes the strip get planted in the railway quarrel and the corn stay corn in the lawyer's; this is Coase's own reasoning (pp. 15–16) but it is applied uniformly; (e) in the smoke quarrel only the works can fit the filter, which stands in for the hundred households' inability to organise as much as for the physics.

This game has had the builder's review only: screenshots and a scripted browser session at 380 px. It has not been played by real strangers, and its length (five to seven minutes) is an estimate.
