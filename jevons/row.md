| **Burn Bright with Jevons** | Jevons (1866), *The Coal Question*, second edition: preface, chapters I, VII and XVIII, and the frontispiece. On Jevons's own ruled paper, set the pace of Britain's coal for ten generations on a ruler that reads both ways, how big the fire gets and how long the coal lasts, and watch it come up out of the ground beneath the line, square by square. New engines come in when they pay; hold the fire level, and the young go over the sea; America passes either way. A ledger keeps what each generation got; the score is the even share your sheet is worth against the best path found, which is drawn on your sheet and turns out to be a plan for the engines. Then the curve continued past 1865 to today, and a coda on the engines Jevons could not see: oil, gas, nuclear power, and wind and sun. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/jevons/burn-bright-with-jevons.html) · [memo](jevons/memo.md) |
