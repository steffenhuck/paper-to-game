THE BALANCE

Homo moralis and the people it replaces

You are evolution's hand. Each society is a village whose people share one temperament: how much they weigh their own gain against doing what would be best if everyone did it. Set the balance. Then newcomers with other temperaments arrive, and either take over or die out.
