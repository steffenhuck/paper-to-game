  SCALES: Tonight you measured on every kind of scale a mind has. The moment of joy had none: a length with nothing to lay it against. Three things from yesterday had an order, longer and shorter, and then a proportion you were asked for and did not know. Your night and your day had a ratio. Your middles had equal steps, and they were not the clock’s: equal steps in you are equal proportions outside, and on such a scale nothing lies endlessly far off. And twice you touched the zero: once to listen for nothing and guess at its length, which no guess could have, once to take half of a voice and double of another from it, and the two did not quite undo each other. The zero is the present, which has no length and is where all of this was measured; and it is the zero Augustine says the world began from, when there was no then.
  BUILDER: Augustine’s problem is the economist’s, because the thing economists most want to measure, how much better off a person is, is also measured in the mind, and the same ladder was climbed there, in the same order, fifteen hundred years later. First the claim that only an order can be known, better or worse, never by how much: that was Pareto’s, and it is your three things. Then the claim that differences can be compared, that going from here to there can be known to be a bigger step than going from there to beyond: that gives equal steps, it was Lange’s and Alt’s, and it is your middles. Then gambles were found to pin the steps down, which was von Neumann’s and Morgenstern’s. And then the one thing nobody could supply: a zero, because no experience is a zero of experience, so “twice as well off” never got a meaning. Time has what utility lacks. The present is its zero. Tonight you were handed that zero, you halved and doubled from it, and you found that against it, nothing can be counted.
