How to play

Find out which of Europe's posters would backfire — before they're printed.

Europe has six posters for Benin City. Every fact on them is true. Europe assumes young people here think Europe is richer, and the trip easier, than it really is.

1 · Knock

You have 12 knocks. Tap a poster and the next person on the street answers that poster's question. You choose where to spend your knocks: ask about every poster, or ask more where the answers disagree.

2 · Read the notes

The truth is worse than they believed: the poster warns them.
The truth is better than they believed: the poster backfires and makes leaving look better.
They knew already, or don't know. Nothing new.

3 · Stamp

PRINT puts a poster on the walls. SCRAP keeps it off. You can stamp at any time, even without asking anyone.

4 · The whole survey

Then the real survey of 555 homes comes in, and every poster is judged: does it warn more people than it backfires on?

The people at the doors are made up. Their answers are drawn from what the real survey found.

About this game

Beber, B. & Scacco, A. (2022). The Myth of the Misinformed Migrant? Survey Insights from Nigeria's Irregular Migration Epicenter. Ruhr Economic Papers No. 957, RWI Essen. doi:10.4419/96973121

Game built from this paper; the paper is the authority where the two disagree.
