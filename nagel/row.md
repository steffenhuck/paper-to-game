| **Zoom Out with Nagel** | Nagel (1974), *The Philosophical Review* 83(4):435–450. What is it like to be a bat? As a 1972 instant camera whose zoom ring steps back from your own eyes: the ring turns freely and the picture changes under your thumb, the colour draining, things leaving the frame and other things coming in, and the shutter fires at three stops (naked eye, the shape, the physics). Four readers who mostly lack your senses ask about four things from the paper. Zoom out on lightning for the eyeless Martian and the bolt stays in the middle and gains the cloud, the ground and the charge; zoom out on red for Ada, blind from birth, and the red leaves the poppies before any frame she can read arrives. Then the bat holds the camera, its naked-eye frame comes to you blank, and you say what is in it.
