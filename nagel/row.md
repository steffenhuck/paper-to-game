| **Zoom Out with Nagel** | Nagel (1974), *The Philosophical Review* 83(4):435–450. What is it like to be a bat? As a 1972 instant camera: a zoom ring with three stops that steps back from your own eyes (from inside, the shape, the physics), and six readers who ask about six things from the paper and mostly lack your senses. Zoom out on lightning for the eyeless Martian and the whole of it comes into the frame; zoom out on red for Ada, blind from birth, and the only frames she can read are the ones the red has left; send the Martian physiologist the physics of your pain and all of your brain arrives, and none of the pain. Then the bat holds the camera, its inside frame comes to you blank, and you say what is in it. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/nagel/zoom-out-with-nagel.html) · [memo](nagel/memo.md) |
