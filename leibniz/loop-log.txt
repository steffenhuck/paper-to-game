=== VERDICT ===
Two of your three worlds are as full as they can be.
Leibniz: the world that exists is the arrangement of possible things that fits the most together, like tiles filling a board. Not everything possible can exist; the grandest thing need not be in the best world; the small, awkward things are; and a step back can be the way to a fuller world.
World 1: your world was full, with the bar of three left out.
World 2: you kept the big square, and your world was about a fifth dark.
World 3: you stepped back two times, and the world ended full: the step back was the way forward.
On world 2 (7 cells short), the best possible world holds more than yours.
A world with less in it than there could have been. Leibniz says that cannot be the one that exists: the possibles push, and the fullest arrangement is what comes through, the way weights settle.
