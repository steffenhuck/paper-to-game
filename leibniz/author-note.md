# Leibniz's Worlds — note for the author

1. Look first at `screenshot-a-4-dark.png` (the covered world with the flame), `screenshot-a-4-twin.png` (the twin at the end), `screenshot-a-4-torch.png` (a torch burning) and `screenshot-a-4-lifting.png` (the cover lifting), `screenshot-a-2-big.png` (the big one lit and what it shut out) and `screenshot-a-3-key-out.png` (the key star put out in world 3). Then play it on a phone; the torch is the thing to judge by eye (how faint, how long: `CONFIG.TORCH`), and the drag of the flame is the thing to feel. Add `?seed=42` to the address to get the draw the screenshots show.
2. Everything tunable is in `CONFIG` at the top of `mechanism.js`, the only copy of the mechanism: the generator's ranges for each world (star counts, weights, pairs, the torch world's holders and distances), the torch's radius, seconds and faintness, the colours, the star radius, the words for how short a world is. Change a range and run `node calibration.js`: it draws two hundred games and tells you whether every lesson still holds on every draw. Then `python3 build.py`, which runs the check again and refuses to build on a failure or a mechanism mismatch.
3. What we were least sure about: (a) no numbers during play: sizes carry the weights (radius 9 + 2.6 × weight), and the status line still gives the total light, so a player who wants arithmetic can get some; (b) the torch's faintness is set for a dark room; on a phone in daylight the lines may vanish, and the numbers in `CONFIG.TORCH` are where to turn; the ends of the big star's lines lie 150–205 px from it against a torch radius of 115, so a torch on the big star never reaches one; (c) which of the twin worlds is played is drawn at random, the other is shown at the end; the salt mine has two such rounds; (c2) the score counts a salt-mine world at what it held after the act, so leaving a full world scores full and choosing the other star in it loses the small star; (d) world 3 now needs the player to light what the step back frees, since nothing comes in on its own; the result line says so when they do not; (e) the title is a dummy; (f) About says the translation is quoted "with his permission" on the strength of Strickland's nod.

This game has had the builder's review only: screenshots and a scripted browser session at 380 px on two seeds. It has not been played by real strangers, and its length has not been measured.
