# Singer's Chalk Line — note for the author

Revision of 25 November 2026, built against the 1974 paper (memo, section 20). The first build, from the 2004 anthology excerpt, is in `previous/`.

1. Look first at `screenshot-one-reason-chalked.png` (the chimpanzee second, three red circles), `screenshot-one-aware-chalked.png` (the mouse above the infant), `screenshot-one-suffer-chalked.png` (the moment), `screenshot-one-race.png` (the paper's own exercise), `screenshot-two-offer.png` (the experimenter's four, and no one), `screenshot-two-q-why.png`, `screenshot-two-result.png` (the species path) and `screenshot-end.png`. Then play it on a phone as someone who wants to keep eating meat: the things to judge are whether the experimenter's three questions feel like the paper asking or like a trap closing, and whether "simple discrimination" lands as the paper's phrase.
2. Everything tunable is in `CONFIG` at the top of `mechanism.js`: the ten beings with their four readings (0–10, each tagged with its page), the four rulers and their one-line descriptions, the mirror's two words, the experiment's subjects and the two reasons offered, the footnote text, and the seven orderings the paper states, which the calibration checks. Change a reading and run `node calibration.js`; then `python3 build.py`, which runs the check again and refuses to build on a failure.
3. What we were least sure about: the two lying figures (the infant and the brain-damaged adult) at row size; the player who takes the infant and is asked nothing; the name "Aware"; the stone reading "none" everywhere.
4. The game has had the builder's review only: screenshots at phone width and two scripted sessions in a headless browser (`session-test.py`). No stranger has played this build.
