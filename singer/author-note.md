# Draw a Line with Singer — note for the author

Revision 12 of 28 November 2026 (memo, sections 27 to 31): three experiments with three fixed menus of three, the nine creatures that can suffer once each (the third menu is the paper's own test: the baby, the dog, the grown-up, to save a thousand lives); the board says how many rules with a ranking can make sense of the three choices (each experiment on its own); then four rules come one at a time, Reason, Talk, Aware and Suffer, and before each speaks the player taps the experiments he expects it to complain about, then Check; the reveal marks cutoffs and circles the worst pair per experiment, with a one-line tally. Suffer's marks are all equal, so it complains wherever some were used and some spared. Then the name, and a verdict that draws the player's line and Singer's. No mirror. A player who would use nobody is sent to Foot.

1. Look first at `screenshot-phone-species-exp1.png`, `-did.png` (the three choices and the count), `-guess.png` (the question before the reveal), `-reason.png` (Reason objects in experiments 2 and 3), `-suffer.png` (the fourth rule, full bars, three complaints), `-name.png`, `-end.png` (the two drawings); the laptop versions have the same names with `laptop`. Then play it four ways: animals used, people spared (Species); nobody for a child's year, everyone from one life on (Singer's line); nobody ever (Foot); the mouse, then the hurt grown-up, then the baby (a Reason line). The things to judge: whether the Suffer screen lands without asking anything; whether the verdict drawings say enough on their own; whether the rule screens now feel like play.
2. Everything tunable is in `CONFIG` at the top of `mechanism.js`: the nine (and the stone, kept in the cast for the Suffer screen and the verdict drawing), the three menus, the three rungs with their stories, the cost, the four rules, and the seven orderings the paper states. The test is `explainsColumn()`; the words for each shape are in `shapeWords()` and the verdict in `verdict()`. Change anything and run `node calibration.js`; then `python3 build.py`; `python3 session-test.py` plays five sessions at two widths and refreshes the screenshots; `node loop.js` narrates one play headlessly.
3. What we were least sure about: the Suffer screen as a picture alone; the verdict icons on a phone; the one-line Species sentence.
4. The game has had the builder's review only. No stranger has played this build.
