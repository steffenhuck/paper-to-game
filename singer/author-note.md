# Draw a Line with Singer — note for the author

Revision 2 of 26 November 2026, built for a smart eleven-year-old on your play notes (memo, section 21). The 2004-excerpt build is in `previous/`.

1. Look first at `screenshot-one-reason-preview.png` (the red dashed line before Chalk it), `screenshot-one-suffer-chalked.png` (the line by hand, with the yellow bracket), `screenshot-one-hand-chalked.png` (the hand round, the line named), `screenshot-laptop-one-hand-chalked.png` (the hand round when the player draws Singer's line, at laptop width), `screenshot-two-q1.png` (why not the baby?) and `screenshot-laptop-end.png`. Then play it as the eleven-year-old: the things to judge are whether "your hand drew that, not the ruler" reads as the ruler failing, whether the hand round feels like your own line, and whether the four answers to "why not the baby?" include the one you would have given.
2. Everything tunable is in `CONFIG` at the top of `mechanism.js`: the ten beings with their four readings (0–10, each tagged with its page) and their on-screen words, the four rulers, the mirror's two words, the experiment's subjects and four answers, and the seven orderings the paper states, which the calibration checks. Change a reading and run `node calibration.js`; then `python3 build.py`, which runs the check again and refuses to build on a failure. `python3 session-test.py` plays five sessions in headless Chromium and refreshes the screenshots.
3. What we were least sure about: "by hand" as a child's idea; the hand round for a player who draws Singer's own line; "who cannot learn much"; whether the four answers are the whole menu.
4. The game has had the builder's review only: screenshots at phone and laptop width and five scripted sessions. No stranger has played this build.
