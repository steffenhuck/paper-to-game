# Draw a Line with Singer — note for the author

Revision 8 of 27 November 2026 (memo, section 27): a new shape. You are the scientist first: three experiments of rising stakes (a child's year of illness, one life, a thousand lives), one of three stories per rung drawn each play, the same cost every time (a day of bad pain, no lasting harm), the paper's four subjects on offer; you tick everyone you would be willing to use. The board reads your line off what you did (for each subject, the smallest good for which you would use it, or never). Then three rules with marks, Reason, Talk, Aware, each checks the order of use and agrees or objects with the two it objects to circled; then "can they suffer?" for the four, and the Suffer rule's complaint built from your own two answers; then the name, the sponge with Race and Sex, Singer's answer, and a verdict about what you did. A player who would use nobody is sent to Foot.

1. Look first at `screenshot-phone-species-exp1.png` (an experiment), `screenshot-phone-species-line.png` (your line), `screenshot-phone-species-reason.png` (Reason objects), `screenshot-phone-species-ask.png` and `-suffer.png` (the question and the Suffer rule's complaint), `-name.png`, `-race.png`, `-end.png`; the laptop versions have the same names with `laptop`. Then play it four ways: animals first and people only for a thousand lives (Species); all four at the same rung (Singer's line); nobody ever (Foot); the hurt grown-up first, then the baby and the mouse, then the chimpanzee (a Reason line). The things to judge: whether the three experiments are quick and easy for an eleven-year-old; whether the rule screens read without the player having to do anything on them; whether the verdict says what you did.
2. Everything tunable is in `CONFIG` at the top of `mechanism.js`: the four subjects, the three rungs with their stories, the cost, the three rules, the mirror's two words, and the seven orderings the paper states. The words for each shape are in `shapeWords()` and the verdict in `verdict()`. Change anything and run `node calibration.js`; then `python3 build.py`; `python3 session-test.py` plays five sessions at two widths and refreshes the screenshots; `node loop.js` narrates one play headlessly.
3. What we were least sure about: the top rung (is a thousand lives enough to move most players to tick a person?); the verdict's length; the Talk rule's tie objection.
4. The game has had the builder's review only. No stranger has played this build.
