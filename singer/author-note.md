# Draw a Line with Singer — note for the author

Revision 7 of 27 November 2026 (memo, sections 23 to 26): the question is the paper's, per unit of pain (hurt exactly as much, matters exactly as much?); you answer with no rule on the board; then three rules with marks, Reason, Talk, Aware, each said out loud when it arrives, each agreeing with your line or objecting and naming the two it objects to, checked again as you move people; then one more question with no marks, Bentham's, can they suffer?, asked only about the beings you count for less, and the Suffer rule's complaints built from your own two answers; the board names your last line and says who moved you; the experiment reads the last line and the verdict always speaks to it. Play again is the revision.
