How to play

Draw the line: who counts, and how much.

The first board

Eleven beings are on the board, every one an example from the paper: an adult, a baby, an infant born with massive brain damage, an adult with a severe intellectual disability, an elder in advanced senility, a chimpanzee, a dog, a pig, a horse, a mouse, and an oak. The aim here: draw one line that keeps every person in and every animal out, with a ruler you can read.

Tap a ruler in the tray: Reasoning, Plans, Ties or Pain. Each sorts the eleven by one thing the paper measures them on, highest at the top, with a yellow mark for each; the marks follow the paper's rankings. Tap a row and a faint line appears under it: everyone above it is in (counts), everyone below is out. Two with the same mark cannot be parted; the line goes under both. Tap Chalk it and the line is drawn for good. The board then circles in red every person left out and every animal let in, and says what this ruler cannot do. Sponge wipes the line; the smear stays. Each ruler is used once; after the four there is one ruler left.

The second board

Four scenes from the paper, two beings each. Twice there is one dose of painkiller: a red mark under each shows how much it hurts now, a yellow mark its Plans mark from the first board. Twice there is a fire and one rescue: only the Plans marks. Tap one, then Give the dose or Pull out. The board says what you did, then what the paper would do and why.

The verdict

What the paper says; what you found on the first board, ruler by ruler; what you chose on the second; where you part from the paper; and what would follow had your way stood. No score.
