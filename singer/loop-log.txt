== CODA. And killing? The paper argues about suffering, not killing. Killing is a footnote. It might be argued, the footnote says, that part of what is wrong with killing a human is that most humans have desires and purposes that reach into the future. If one took that view, the footnote goes on, one would have to hold that killing an infant or a human who is permanently retarded is less serious than killing a higher mammal that has a sense of its own existence over time. The paper leaves it there. So does this board.
