== BOARD TWO. Now you are the scientist. One experiment would save many lives. It needs one subject: someone it is done to, and it will hurt.

   You: chimp
   Singer asks: Why not the baby?  [Because the baby is one of us. | Because the baby will grow into more than the chimpanzee. | Because the chimpanzee matters less. | No reason. Either would have done.]
   You: human
   Board: You used the chimpanzee, not the baby, because the baby is one of us.
