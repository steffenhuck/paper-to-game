| **Meitner's Drop** | Meitner & Frisch (1939), *Nature* 143:239–240. Disintegration of uranium by neutrons: a new type of nuclear reaction. A manga page: you are the uranium drop, barely holding, and the chemists have a shelf of test tubes from four years of neutrons, five of them filed as elements beyond uranium on their 1939 table. Drag the line to choose where to cut: a cut in the middle always works, one further out may fail, and a failed cut leaves the drop holding its neutron. The pieces fly apart, land on the table and change by beta decay, and a tube lights when a piece stands in its column. Barium forces krypton; a piece near masurium climbs through all five "transuranic" columns and the "new elements" come down; only a failed cut, a drop that kept its neutron, gives the 24-minute body. Then thorium. Four neutrons; verdict in words. Rev. 2 (26 Nov): the odds, on the author's note that fission came too easily. Rev. 5 (27 Nov): says what you will learn whether the drop splits or the cut fails. Rev. 4 (26 Nov): one idea per screen: the cut goes anywhere on a hill of chances, the game shows what a cut will light before you fire, and a failed cut lights the 24-minute tube. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/meitner/meitners-drop.html) · [memo](meitner/memo.md) |
