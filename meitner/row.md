| **Meitner's Drop** | Meitner & Frisch (1939), *Nature* 143:239–240. Disintegration of uranium by neutrons: a new type of nuclear reaction. A manga page: you are the uranium drop, barely holding, and the chemists have a shelf of test tubes from four years of neutrons, five of them filed as elements beyond uranium on their 1939 table. Drag the line to choose where you pinch, or hold together; the pieces fly apart with about 200 Mev, land on the table and climb by beta decay, and a tube lights when a piece stands in its column. Barium forces krypton; a piece near masurium climbs through all five "transuranic" columns and the ghosts come down; only the drop that holds gives the 24-minute body. Then thorium.
