# Ostrom's Pond — note for the author

1. Look first at `screenshot-a-3-result.png` (summer three, the first watched summer: Ada on the stool, Cyd and Jem told off and nothing else happening to them, Gus not seen), then `screenshot-a-7-result.png` (the occasional policeman: nobody told off, "that's his job now"), then `screenshot-a-end.png` and `screenshot-b-end.png` (the eleventh summer after watching, and after ten summers of the daily policeman). Then play it once on a phone; `#v=1234` on the URL replays that village.
2. Everything tunable is in `CONFIG` at the top of `mechanism.js`, the only copy of the mechanism: the type shares, the pond's breeding, the choice weights (temptation, reciprocity, base reluctance, noise), the five arrangements (promise pull, shame, assurance, the watcher's chance of seeing, the believed chance before and after a telling-off, the graduated fines and what a sanction teaches, the town's fine, the two policemen's chances and fees), the winter (imitation, reinforcement, being suckered, grabbing, crowding out under each policeman), and the newcomer. Edit, run `node calibration.js` (43 checks, one sensitivity line, and the play-it-in-numbers table), then `python3 build.py`, which refuses to build if the embedded mechanism differs from the file. `node sweep-test.js 8` plays eight villages with random cards in a headless browser and checks, after every summer, that what the game says agrees with what it draws and records.
3. What we were least sure about: (a) the whole thing is a toy, and its sizes are ours: the paper gives directions and reported numbers from other studies, not a model; (b) the daily policeman's eleventh summer falls below leave-it-be's only if crowding out runs at least three-quarters of the rate chosen (the sensitivity line in `calibration-result.txt`); the verdict screen says the result depends on how fast a norm withers; (c) the occasional policeman starts a little above leave-it-be, because a small deterrent is still a deterrent, and ends level with it with the norm gone; the paper's "worst of all worlds" is read as worst against the village's own rules and against strong enforcement, which holds; (d) under "have a talk" the egoists eat double for ten summers and the pond holds, so a village that talks eats more than one that watches; the verdict does not rank, and the paper's line about trust alone is on the talk verdict; (e) the newcomer is always an egoist and the leaver always a norm-user, our reading of the paper's in-migration and out-migration threats; (f) every sanction is in kind (a word; both fish back into the pond; both fish back and a summer out; both fish to the policeman), so that the hands in the row always show what was eaten; the deterrent sizes are therefore two fish and a summer's fishing, not a tariff.

This game has had the builder's review only: screenshots and a scripted browser session at 380 px. It has not been played by real strangers, and its length is an estimate.
