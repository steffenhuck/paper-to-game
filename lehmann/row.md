The paper that put a core within the Earth's core, as a lit geological plate. Two possibilities and the records to judge them by: every listening post felt the earthquake, four of them only faintly. You are the wave. Eight dives into an Earth with one core, and no wave reaches the four faint posts, with a jump of sixty degrees between a dive of 34° and one of 33½°. Then four dives into an Earth with a core within the core, where a steep wave is bent or bounced into the shadow. Which Earth fits what the posts recorded?
