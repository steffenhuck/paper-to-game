| **Lehmann's Shadow** | Inge Lehmann (1936), "P′", *Publ. Bureau Central Séismologique International* A14:87–115, read from the original, with Kölbl-Ebert (2001), *Episodes* 24(4):262–267. The paper that put a ball inside the Earth's core, as a lit geological plate: you are an earthquake wave, you choose how steeply to dive into Lehmann's two-shell Earth, and you watch yourself run straight and bend at the core and come up somewhere on the rim where eighteen listening posts stand. After six waves one post is ringed and you are asked to hit it; nobody can, and the fan shows why: a band no dive reaches, with a jump of sixty degrees between a dive of 34° and one of 33½°. Then what three real posts in the shadow wrote in 1929, and twelve more waves into the same Earth, where a steep dive is bent by something that is not drawn, leaves a white mark on its edge, and comes up inside the shadow. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/lehmann/lehmanns-shadow.html) · [memo](lehmann/memo.md) |
