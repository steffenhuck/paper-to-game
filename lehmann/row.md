| **Lehmann's Shadow** | Kölbl-Ebert (2001), *Episodes* 24(4):262–267, on Inge Lehmann (1936), "P′", *Publ. Bureau Central Séismologique International* A14:87–115. The paper that put a ball inside the Earth's core, as a lit geological plate: you are an earthquake wave, you choose how steeply to dive into Lehmann's two-shell Earth, and you watch yourself run straight and bend at the core and come up somewhere on the rim where eighteen listening posts stand. Your shots build her own figure, and four posts turn out to sit in a band no angle can reach, with a jump of sixty degrees between a dive of 34° and one of 33½°. Then the posts that heard something anyway: build her inner ball with two dials, its size and its speed against the core's, fire a quake at every angle, and see the waves bounce off it into the shadow. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/lehmann/lehmanns-shadow.html) · [memo](lehmann/memo.md) |
