Bureau Central Séismologique International* A14:87–115, read from the original, with Kölbl-Ebert (2001), *Episodes* 24(4):262–267. The paper that put a core within the Earth's core, as a lit geological plate. First you watch a real earthquake: most listening posts light up, four only quiver. Then you are the earthquake. Eight goes in an Earth with one core, where no direction makes those four do anything, with a jump of sixty degrees between a dive of 34° and one of 33½°. Then four goes in an Earth with a core within the core, where a steep dive comes up in the shadow and a post quivers. Every direction at once closes each stage: dead, then quivering, as recorded.
