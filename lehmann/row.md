Bureau Central Séismologique International* A14:87–115, read from the original, with Kölbl-Ebert (2001), *Episodes* 24(4):262–267. The paper that put a core within the Earth's core, as a lit geological plate. It starts from the facts: three real earthquakes, and each time most listening posts light up while one band only quivers. Then imagine an Earth with one core and be the earthquake: four goes and one last try to make the band quiver, with a jump of sixty degrees between a dive of 34° and one of 33½°, and then every direction at once, the band silent, and the game's apology for the trap. Then imagine a core within the core: three goes, and a steep dive makes the band quiver.
