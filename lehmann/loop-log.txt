VERDICT (two)
  You found the inner core.
  You were the earthquake. In an Earth with one core, none of your waves could reach the four faint posts, and no dive ever can: they sit in a shadow. In an Earth with a core within the core, steep waves are turned aside by the inner core and come up inside that shadow. Two of your four waves did just that, and you reached the faint post at 120°. You chose the Earth with a core within the core. It is the one that fits.
  --
  That is what Inge Lehmann worked out in 1936, by hand, with a pencil. Real listening posts inside the shadow had felt a faint wave. An Earth with one core could not explain it. An Earth with a small core inside the core, where waves run faster, could. She did not say she had proved it. She called it an idea that fitted the records better, and asked others to check.
  --
  They checked, and she was right. The inner core is about 1220 km from its centre to its edge: smaller than the Moon. Lehmann never said what it is made of. Others worked that out later, and by 1971 it was clear: a solid ball of iron, inside a core of liquid iron.
  "inside the core there is an inner core in which the velocity is larger"
