    How to play

    How to play

    Read the news on air. Tap once for every syllable — get every word out.

    The prompter

    Each word of the script appears on the black prompter, one word per beat. The dots under it show its syllables: PAS·SEN·GERS has three, so tap three times before the beat runs out. The white bar along the bottom of the prompter is the beat running out. The next word waits underneath.

    The pad

    Tap the big pad at the bottom. Two thumbs work. A word you tap right lights up amber: you got it out. Too few taps, or two too many, and it comes out as mush.

    Breath

    A full stop gives you a rest beat. A long sentence leaves you out of breath: the breath bar runs down and the beat gets quicker until the next full stop.

    After each item

    A block of flats lights one window for every twentieth of the words you got out. Then you see what the script asked of you, and what the study found about writers like this one.

    Tonight

    Five items on one story — trains running late — from a tabloid, the UK government, the European Commission, a professor, and Brussels’ own archive. The end screen tells you what the study found.
