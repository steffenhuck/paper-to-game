| **Beam Me Up, Leibniz** | Leibniz (c. 1702–1705), *On Unities and Transmigration*, LBr 40 Bl. 22–23, in Lloyd Strickland's translation (2016). Can a soul move from one body to another? A 1960s transfer station: you in a pod, a far station, a beam. Press it: a pilot appears over there who remembers your morning, and you are still here. Who arrived? Then two ways to fix having two of you: fire (a pod under every layer, past a million) and a swap (no sign, and nothing is written; a lamp, and the pod's own log explains it). Then book your passage, by beam or by pod; a verdict in words.
