| **Beam Me Up, Leibniz** | Leibniz (c. 1702–1705), *On Unities and Transmigration*, LBr 40 Bl. 22–23, in Lloyd Strickland's translation (2016). Can a soul move from one body to another? A 1960s transfer station: you in a pod, a far station. The station tries four ways to get you there (move, so burn the pod open: a pod under every layer; swap: nothing anywhere is different; swap with a lamp: the pod's own timer flashed it; beam: the far pilot wakes with your life in its head, and you are still here), and after each asks you one thing: where are you now? Then Leibniz answers. Book your passage by beam or by pod; a verdict in words.
