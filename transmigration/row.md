| **Beam Me Up, Leibniz** | Leibniz (c. 1702–1705), *On Unities and Transmigration*, LBr 40 Bl. 22–23, in Lloyd Strickland's translation (2016). Could you leave your body and move into another one? A 1960s transfer station tries three ways to get you to the far station: get you out (tap your pod to burn a layer: a pod inside a pod, the station gives up past a million), swap (nothing anywhere is different; then pick a sign, and the far pod's clock shows it would have done that anyway), and the beam (the far pilot wakes with your whole life in its head, and you are still here). After every try you tap the pod you are in; then Leibniz answers. Travel on by beam or by pod. Written for a smart 11-year-old.
