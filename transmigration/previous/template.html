Transfer station · a game from Leibniz, c. 1702

Beam Me Up, Leibniz

Pods drift in the dark, a pilot in each. Every pod has a porthole, and in every porthole the whole fleet, seen from where that pod is. Under the pad run two tapes, the pod's log and the pilot's, in step, with no wire between them.

Your hands will set a fire and turn a dial, swap two pilots and watch the portholes, pick a sign for a pod to show, and in the end book your own passage.

How to play

The aim: get a pilot out of one pod and into another, and get the station's book to record it.

What is on the pad

Pods in the dark, a pilot in each: the dog in pod A, the cat in pod B, the fern, a mouse, and a diamond with no porthole. Every pod has a porthole, and in it the whole fleet from where that pod is. Under the pad, two tapes: the pod's log (what happens to it, line by line, each line from the line before: the timer, the thruster, the heater) and the pilot's tape (what the pilot feels at each line). There is no wire between them; they agree. The station's book, the amber strip, writes what the pods show and nothing else.

Shift 1: the dog

Three keys. Touch one and its settings appear with a preview on the pad; then press the chrome key to do it. Every try plays out one step at a time; press Next. After the last step the station says in its own words what happened, then the book writes its line, then Leibniz has his say.

  • Burn: set how deep (one layer, ten, a hundred, all the way) and press Fire. The outside of pod A burns off, layer by layer, and you see what is under each.
  • Swap quietly: pick a pod to swap the dog with (the cat's pod, the fern's pod, the diamond) and press Swap. Two shadows cross; then the portholes and the tapes are shown before and after.
  • Swap with a mark: pick a sign for pod B to show when the dog arrives (its lamp, a roll, a new course) and press Swap. Pod B shows the sign; its log writes it; the book reads the line before.

Each key can be tried more than once with another setting. The shift ends when all three have been tried; then press End the shift.

Shift 2: you

You sit in your pod with a stick. Push it left or right and the pod rolls; both tapes say why. Then pull the stick out, want a turn, and press To the console. Then one key, Beam, with a sign for the far pod to show (its lamp, a roll, a memory); press Beam. Then Book your passage to the far station: by beam, by pod, or by beam and then fire for the pod left here. Pick a way, read what it does, press Book passage.

The end

No score. The verdict says what Leibniz found, what you found, where the two part, and what would have followed had yours stood.
