| **Dear Household** | Ganguli & Mengel (2026), *Journal of Public Economics* 253:105558. Social influence and carbon dioxide mitigation: does knowing that someone will see your choice make you give more to tree planting, and how far does it carry? As a 1970s public-information poster: you are a town's planner, twelve streets of twenty households, ten pounds each for saplings, and one letter per pair of streets saying who will see each household's pledge and when: the sheet to everyone, one spokesperson to all, pass it on to one, afterwards, the clipboard, or nothing. Plant the street, bet on what the next street plants when it sees what you sent, and find that the humble letter is the only one that works and the ripple is less than a sapling. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/ganguli/dear-household.html) · [memo](ganguli/memo.md) |
