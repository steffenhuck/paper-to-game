What we were least sure about: (a) the spokesperson letter realises INF-(20,1) in a closed street as one household seen by the twenty on the next street, with nineteen told nothing; your design gave every observed person twenty observers; (b) the extensive margin, OBS-(1,1) and INF-(1,20) are played as exactly zero where your point estimates are +0.131, −0.014 and −0.019 against baseline, so that no dead letter looks alive or dead by a rounding; the points are in the config behind the played values; (c) the next street's copy is Table 4's slope added to a reader's own plain pledge and centred on the plain pledge, where your constant puts a reader of a plain pledge eight pence lower; readers are never also told who sees them; (d) the histograms are read off the figure and adjusted to your means; they decide only how plots look; (e) the bet's cutoffs in saplings (accurate within 2, mild to 6, substantial beyond) are ours, since Appendix Figure C.2 was not available; the verdict quotes your type shares beside them; (f) each street is an expected street with no sampling noise, which the About screen says, since a real street of twenty would swing by more than the effect.

This game has had the builder's review only: screenshots and a scripted browser session at 380 px. It has not been played by real strangers, and its length is an estimate.
