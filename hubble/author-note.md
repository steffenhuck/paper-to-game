# Hubble's Twelve Nights — note for the author

1. Look first at `screenshot-eyepiece.png` (the split field with the halves matched) and `screenshot-spectrograph-virgo.png` (the bracket on the bars, the ghost outline where they would sit at rest), then `screenshot-plate-first-far.png` (the first Virgo nebula landing far right, with Hubble's dotted dots under the player's) and `screenshot-humason-spectrum.png` (NGC 7619's bars a long way to the red).
2. Everything tunable is in the `CONFIG` block at the top of `mechanism.js`, the only copy of the mechanism: Table 1 with the catalogue coordinates, Hubble's solar motion and his two K values, the brightest-star constant −6.3, the H and K wavelengths and the spectrograph's window, the twelve nights and the one/two/three-night costs, the dial ranges and the two tolerance rings. Edit, run `node calibration.js` (50 checks against the paper and the game's framing), then `python3 build.py`, which refuses to finish if the embedded mechanism differs from the file.
3. What we were least sure about: (a) the costs and the budget. Twelve nights with 1/2/3-night prices make the far nebulae expensive, which is the deliberation; the heuristics table in the memo shows the decision matters (Virgo-only shows nothing, near-only shows the relation only after the Sun's motion comes off, a mix shows it best). (b) The eyepiece reads every nebula by the brightest-star rule, including the six Hubble did by Cepheids and the four Virgo nebulae he did by cluster brightness; the About screen says so. (c) The paper's tables were transcribed from a scan with a bad OCR layer; the numbers were checked against the standard transcriptions of this paper and the calibration recovers Hubble's own solution from them, but a second pair of eyes on the 24 rows would be welcome.

Revised once after a first play (memo, section 19): split-field eyepiece, bars and bracket in the spectrograph, a self-running first night, Hubble's dots after each reading, a plate that describes and a verdict that concludes.

This game has had the builder's review and one play only: screenshots and a scripted browser session at 380 px. It has not been played by real strangers, and the five-minute length is an estimate.
