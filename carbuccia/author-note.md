# The Crèche Counter — note for the authors

1. Look first at `screenshot-a-ward.png` (the ward with no help: the gap widening down the stairs), then `screenshot-a-1-result.png` (the videos: the bars sit on their ghosts), `screenshot-a-2-result.png` (calls to the 39 mothers without a degree: the orange APPLIED bar past its ghost, the place gap at 30), `screenshot-a-3-target.png` (calls to the fifteen who knew least, seen as the ones you called against the rest) and `screenshot-a-end.png`. Then play it once on a phone.
2. Everything tunable is in `CONFIG` at the top of `mechanism.js`, the only copy of the mechanism: the control means and intention-to-treat effects by group from Table 2 and Extended Data Tables 3 and 4, the first stage, the exploratory subgroups from the text and read off Figures 5 and 6, the costs, and everything ours (the ward of 100, the subgroup shares and cross-shares, the purse, the four years, the words). Edit, run `node calibration.js` (72 checks and the play-it-in-numbers table), then `python3 build.py`, which refuses to build if the embedded mechanism differs from the file.
3. What we were least sure about: (a) a targeted order applies your conditional ITT to the targeted group alone, with no spillover and nothing carried between years: the trial treated whole arms, and nothing in it says what targeting does; (b) the shares of the low-knowledge and not-in-work mothers who are without a degree or born abroad are ours, as is the low-knowledge group's size (15 of 100); the calibration check shows the central discoveries do not depend on them, but the "ones you called" view for those two targets does; (c) the videos are played as exactly zero where your point estimates are a point or two below it, so that videos aimed at one group do not widen a gap by a rounding of a null; (d) the exploratory low-knowledge effect on places, converted to ITT by the first stage, is the one order in the game that visibly moves places, and the game says on the result screen that it is a small group; (e) baseline intention for mothers born in France and with a degree is derived from the text's low-SES and migrant shares and Figure 2's gaps, not read from a table.

This game has had the builder's review only: screenshots and a scripted browser session at 380 px. It has not been played by real strangers, and its length is an estimate.
