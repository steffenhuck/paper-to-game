| **The Crèche Counter** | Carbuccia, Heim, Barone & Chevallier (2025), *Nature Human Behaviour* 10:64–79. A randomised trial in Paris maternity wards: do information videos or a helper on the phone get low-income and immigrant families the early childcare places they want? You are the fund, four years running, with a ward of a hundred expectant mothers each year and the paper's two tools at the paper's prices: five videos for €5.50 a mother, or a helper who phones and fills in the forms for €55. Pick a tool, pick whom it goes to, send it, and watch the year run on a ticket-counter board: the videos change nothing; the calls get the mothers without a degree to apply nearly as often as the others; and the town hall gives out the places as before. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/carbuccia/the-creche-counter.html) · [memo](carbuccia/memo.md) |
