VERDICT
  The gap in applications narrowed by about a third in the years you called the mothers who were falling behind; the gap in places hardly moved.
  You spent €9,020 over four years: videos to everyone, then calls to mothers without a degree, then calls to mothers who know least about crèches, then calls to everyone.
  The paper found the same. Five videos changed nothing it could measure. A helper on the phone got mothers without a degree, and mothers born abroad, to apply nearly as often as the others. Places did not follow, except for the few who knew least about the system; and the crèche places the calls did win went mostly to mothers with a degree.
  What stands between an application and a place is not in the mother’s hands: too few places, and the town hall’s rules for giving them out, which favour parents who are both in work.
