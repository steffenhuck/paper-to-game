# Watson's Cardboard — note for the author

1. Look first at `screenshot-welcome.png` (the story), then `screenshot-ladder-zipped-guesser.png` (a first-timer's ladder: the rails bulge at G–G and pinch at C–T, with the benchmark line), then `screenshot-end-both.png` and `screenshot-end-guesser.png` for the two kinds of verdict.
2. Every number is in the `CONFIG` block at the top of `mechanism.js`, the only copy of the mechanism: the letter's helix numbers (p. 737), the bump/dent pattern of each base, the piece lengths (our units), the ladder size, the benchmarks from the heuristic simulation and the pace of the pulled-apart animation. Edit, run `node calibration.js` (30 checks, including the heuristic players), then `python3 build.py`, which refuses to finish if the embedded mechanism differs from the file.
3. What we were least sure about: (a) the bump/dent patterns are standard chemistry for the keto forms, not printed in the letter, and the game uses the letter's two contacts per pair (G–C has a third); (b) the welcome story is Watson's own account in *The Double Helix*, ch. 26, retold from memory and not checked against the book; (c) the copying goes beyond this letter (it follows the second 1953 letter), and the About screen says so.

This game has had the builder's review only: screenshots and a scripted browser session at 380 px. It has not been played by real strangers, and the 2–3 minute length is an estimate. The earlier versions (a second ladder, Watson's wrong model to mend, the timed copy, Chargaff's jars) are in the memo and in `previous/`.
