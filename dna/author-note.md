2. Every number is in the `CONFIG` block at the top of `mechanism.js`, the only copy of the mechanism: the letter's helix numbers (p. 737), the bump/dent pattern of each base, the piece lengths (our units), the ladder size, the number of rounds (ROUNDS: 2), the benchmarks from the heuristic simulation. Edit, run `node calibration.js` (34 checks, including the heuristic players, the score and the two rounds), then `python3 build.py`, which refuses to finish if the embedded mechanism differs from the file.
3. What we were least sure about: (a) the bump/dent patterns are standard chemistry for the keto forms, not printed in the letter, and the game uses the letter's two contacts per pair (G–C has a third); (b) the welcome story is Watson's own account in *The Double Helix*, ch. 26, retold from memory and not checked against the book; (c) the copying goes beyond this letter (it follows the second 1953 letter), and the About screen says so.

This game has had the builder's review only: screenshots and a scripted browser session at 380 px. It has not been played by real strangers, and the 3-minute length is an estimate. The earlier versions (a second ladder, Watson's wrong model to mend, the timed copy, Chargaff's jars) are in the memo and in `previous/`.
