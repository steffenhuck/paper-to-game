With a hull of 6 tons, the earth their cannonballs need leaves no room for a cannon of yours, so the enemy cannons stay and the rest wait; at B a foot of earth is enough and the rest cross at once. / LEIBNIZ'S OTHER REMARKS / Against earth boats the enemy can only throw bombs from mortars, which are not easily kept off soldiers; but mortars are not accurate, the enemy will not expect it, and one can land up- or downstream where they cannot follow. Build the boats in a closed yard far from the river, prepare the earth elsewhere, and load only when ready, so that spies cannot learn it in time. Choose a bend with its bulge towards the enemy, so that they must divide to watch it.
