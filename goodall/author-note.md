# Go Fishing with Goodall — note for the author

1. Look first at `screenshot-a-mound-floppy.png`, `screenshot-a-mound-jerk.png` and `screenshot-a-mound-slow.png` (the same afternoon: a floppy grass blade folds at the first bend, a stiff stem pulled out at a flick loses every termite, the same stem drawn out slowly comes up beaded, and Goodall's red pencil ticks each dip on her ruler of ages), then `screenshot-a-hollow-stem.png` (the termite movement at the water) and `screenshot-a-end.png`. Then play it once on a phone, with your thumb: the slow pull is the game.
2. Everything tunable is in `CONFIG` at the top of `mechanism.js`, the only copy of the mechanism: the paper's numbers (the adults' five to eight inches, the three-to-four-year-olds' one or two, the two termites in twenty-two bouts, the ages), each tagged with its page; and everything ours (the passage and its bends, termites per inch, how fast they bite on and how hard they hold, the stems in the long grass, the wear of a tip, the twelve suns, the mother's dips, the sips at the hollow). The words the game says after each dip are in the same file (`dipWords`, `RED`, `verdict`). Edit, run `node calibration.js` (26 checks: the paper's numbers, eight strategies played through whole afternoons, twelve variants of our choices), then `python3 build.py`, which refuses to build if the embedded mechanism differs from the file. The page numbers are from a scanned PDF; the handful of numbers used were read from the printed text, not off a figure, but a check against a clean copy would not hurt.
3. What we were least sure about: (a) the mound drawn cut open, which shows the player what a chimpanzee's fingers only feel, and makes the learning far quicker than the infants' two years (the verdict says so); (b) mapping each dip to one of Goodall's ages, which turns her descriptions into a classifier she never proposed (a floppy stem is always "three to four", a jerk always "two to three"); (c) the hollow, where the infant's grass sponge drinks its fill only by being sucked and chewed five times, and the leaf wad in two: the sips are ours; (d) watching the mother is drawn as a direct lesson (you see her stem, her depth, her pull) where the paper says only that infants watch intently and that observation "might" play a role; (e) the drawings are ours, in the manner of the paper's pencil figures, not traced from them.

This game has had the builder's review only: screenshots and scripted browser sessions at 380 px. It has not been played by real strangers, and its length (about five to six minutes) is an estimate.
