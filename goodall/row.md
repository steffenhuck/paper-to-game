| **Go Fishing with Goodall** | van Lawick-Goodall (1970), *Advances in the Study of Behavior* 3:195–249. Tool-using in primates and other vertebrates, as a field notebook in graphite: you are a young chimpanzee at Gombe, your mother fishing beside you. Take a stem from the long grass, press, and it goes down a termite hole; wait, and let go, while Goodall's red pencil says which age of infant fishes the way you just did (too thick, too floppy, a snatch: two to three; deep in, a wait, gently out: grown-up). Then water in a hollow tree, or driver ants; and every afternoon a different mound, other grass, your mother there or gone ahead. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/goodall/go-fishing-with-goodall.html) · [memo](goodall/memo.md) |
