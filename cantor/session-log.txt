END: One row slipped past its own number and still matched you, so your row does not prove it yet. Cantor's rule never lets a row slip: at every letter, look at the teal line and write the other one. Then every row is different from yours by the time you pass it.
      That is Cantor's argument of 1891. However the endless book is written, and however long it is, this one rule writes a row that is missing from it. So the book can never be complete. Its lines are numbered 1, 2, 3 and on for ever, and still there are more rows than there are numbers. Some infinities are bigger than others.
