== TOUR: Meet the Engine
   The columns of wheels hold numbers. This one, a, holds 5; n holds 7; R is empty, waiting for an answer. | A card can only use two numbers. So to add up 5 + 7 + 5, the Engine needs two cards: the first adds a and n and puts the answer on R; the second takes that answer from R and adds a to it.
  shot tour-start
   step: Card 1: a + n is 5 + 7 = 12. It goes on R.
   step: Card 2: R + a is 12 + 5 = 17. It goes on R.
  shot tour-step
   step: Card 3: the Engine prints 17.
   step: Card 2 used R, the answer from card 1, as one of its two numbers. That is how the Engine does a long sum: one step per card, each card carrying on from the last. And the cards never said “5” or “7”, only “a” and “n”, so they work for any numbers on those wheels.
  shot tour-end
