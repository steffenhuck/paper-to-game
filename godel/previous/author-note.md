# Gödel's Loophole — note for the author

1. Look first at `screenshot-a-3-loophole.png` (the loop drawn round the sentence and what the board says follows), then `screenshot-a-2-composer.png` (the three rows of parts), `screenshot-a-2-praise.png` (the self-praising sentence proved: the live wrong branch), `screenshot-a-4-rb2-found.png` (Rulebook 2's loophole one line down), `screenshot-a-5-omega.png` (the pages refuted one by one), `screenshot-a-6-cert-run.png` (the certificate's chain) and `screenshot-a-end.png`. Then play it on a phone.
2. Everything tunable is in `CONFIG` at the top of `mechanism.js`, the only copy of the mechanism: the five fixed shapes, Gödel's shape, the composer's parts, the budgets (three runs before the composer opens, six in it, three to find Rulebook 2's loophole, four pages), and the `GENEROUS_LEDGER` switch that turns the toy rulebook into the paper's weaker one. Change anything and run `node calibration.js`: it sweeps every sentence the composer can write and checks the paper's structure. Then `python3 build.py`, which runs the check again and refuses to build on a failure or a mechanism mismatch.
3. What we were least sure about: (a) the budget of six composer runs, and whether Gödel's chalk taking over is kind or deflating; (b) the self-praising sentence being proved (Löb's theorem, 1955, not in the paper) as the composer's wrong branch; (c) the toy rulebook reading "refuted" off its own ledger, which the paper's system cannot do (About says so; the check runs the weaker rulebook too); (d) the pages scene is watching rather than deciding, and runs five taps; (e) the certificate round is a bet, the weak form of commitment; (f) the working for a nested sentence can run to thirteen chalk lines.

This game has had the builder's review only: screenshots and a scripted browser session at 380 px. It has not been played by real strangers, and its length has not been measured.
