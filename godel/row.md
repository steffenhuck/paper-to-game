| **Gödel's Loophole** | Gödel (1931), *Monatshefte für Mathematik und Physik* 38:173–198. On formally undecidable propositions of Principia Mathematica and related systems, as chalk on a green board: a rulebook for whole numbers that proves or refutes; five shapes with a hole in them, numbered by their line, two of them about the rulebook itself; fill a hole, run it, and the chalk writes its working and a tick or a cross. Write shape 6 yourself from three parts (proves or does not prove, shape □ or a number, filled with □ or a number) and fill it: the one that says only "I am proved" gets a tick, and the one that says "Rulebook 1 does not prove shape □ filled with □", filled with 6, gets a pink loop that never closes, and is true. Then close the loophole three ways: add it as a rule, and Rulebook 2 gets a line of its own and a loophole at 7; add its opposite, and Rulebook 2′ says a page of the ledger proves it and refutes every page you show it; ask for the certificate that it never contradicts itself, and the chalk shows the certificate would prove the loophole. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/godel/godels-loophole.html) · [memo](godel/memo.md) |
