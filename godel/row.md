| **Gödel's Endgame** | Gödel (1931), *Monatshefte für Mathematik und Physik* 38:173–198. On formally undecidable propositions of Principia Mathematica and related systems, as Hilbert's honest machine and his promise that it prints every sentence about whole numbers or its NOT. Play the machine on sentences with + and × (nonsense gets stamped *not a sentence*); pack 1 + 1 = 2 into one number with primes and unpack another; judge a sentence about the machine itself; then fill one gap so a sentence talks about itself, and find that the machine can neither print it nor its NOT, and that it is true. | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/godel/godels-endgame.html) · [memo](godel/memo.md) |
