A province of France · 1690 to 1700

Leibniz’s
Register

You are the physician of a province. Every year the seasons come in as four cards, a hundred sick come to you with the year’s fever, and you give them one remedy for the year. The same remedy saves them in one kind of year and kills them in another.

Nobody has written the years down. In 1694 a letter from Mr Leibniz will change that.

Read the four cards of the year. Open the register, once there is one. Give the hundred sick one remedy.

Lose as few of the sick as you can in eleven years: each year, give the one remedy that fits the kind of year it is.

1694 · a letter arrives
From the First Doctor of the KingParis, July 1694

Mr Leibniz writes in the Journal des sçavans. In Modena a doctor has kept a history of each year, and in two years has already seen the bark succeed incomparably better in one than in the other. Years like past years come back; a doctor with no record of them learns again from scratch, at the expense of the sick.

From this year, write each year down. This is the register: a few lines a year, in this order:

  • the seasons and the air;
  • the harvest and the beasts;
  • what you gave the sick, and what came of it: what helped, what harmed.

What you wrote you will keep. What you did not write, you will not.

The four years behind you were never written. Your head holds last year whole, the year before it in part, and little beyond that.

The register

How to play

Lose as few of the sick as you can in eleven years: each year, give the one remedy that fits the kind of year it is.

The year

Four cards show the four seasons of the year and their weather: wet (teal, drops), hot (mustard, sun), cold (pale, snowflake), mild (red, cloud). A line beneath says how the harvest and the beasts did. Years of the same kind have the same four cards and the same line.

The remedy

Three remedies: bark, bleeding, rest. In every kind of year one of them helps (five of a hundred die), one does nothing (twenty die), one harms (thirty die). Which is which depends only on the kind of year, and the same remedy can help in one kind and harm in another. Tap a remedy to see the move, then tap Give it to the hundred sick. You see what the remedy you gave did; you never see what the others would have done.

What you have to go on

Three memory cards show the last three years as your head holds them: last year whole, with its cards, its remedy and its dead; two years ago its cards and its remedy, but not what came of it; three years ago its cards, faded. Older years are gone. From 1694, the register holds every year you wrote, whole, for as long as you like: tap it to open it, and look for a past year with the same four cards.

The end

After 1700 the game says what became of the sick, sets your eleven years beside three other doctors (memory alone, the register from 1690, and one who knew every year), and shows the eleven years as they were. Add ?seed= and a number to the address to replay a draw.
