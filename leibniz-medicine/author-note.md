# Leibniz's Register — note for the author (second build)

1. Look first at `screenshot-a-1692-result.png` (the lookalike winter: bark harmed, and the line that names both winters), `screenshot-a-1695-choose.png` (the first winter with the letters: the digest under the cards, five own winters in the strip, none of them this kind), `screenshot-a-1695-collection.png` and `screenshot-a-1698-collection.png` (the collection at thirteen letters and at fifty-two, grouped by kind with the tallies), and `screenshot-a-end.png` (the verdict in two halves, the ladder of five doctors, the ten winters). Then play it on a phone; the thing to judge is whether 1695 lands, when a winter you never had is answered by twelve doctors you never met, and whether the alone years are hard in the right way rather than merely hard. Add `?seed=42` for the draw the screenshots show.
2. Everything tunable is in `CONFIG` at the top of `mechanism.js`, the only copy of the mechanism: the plan of your own winters (`PLAN`, as letters: A any kind, B its lookalike, C and D the others), the letter year, the three counts of the dead, the twenty-four provinces, whether the other physicians read (`OTHERS_READ`), and the two floors on the draw (`FLOOR`: the letters of 1694 must hold your 1695 winter's helper; by 1696 every kind's). Change a thing and run `node calibration.js`; then `python3 build.py`, which runs the check again and refuses to build on a failure or a mechanism mismatch.
3. What we were least sure about: (a) the other physicians read the letters from their second year, so the digest converges fast and the second regime is near certain; the first year's letters keep the harm rows; `OTHERS_READ` in `CONFIG` turns this off; (b) the floors reject about two draws in three to make 1695 and 1696 certain; without them the reader still beats memory by a wide margin (calibration), but a stranger could meet a 1695 with no answer; (c) the collection groups the letters by kind, so the matching is done by the record, as a compiled collection would do it, and the player's job is to find their group and read; if you want the player to match the cards themselves, the raw letters sorted by year are one change in `renderBook`; (d) a fixed remedy does about as well as the own-winters doctor over the ten (memo §18); (e) the link to Strickland's page could not be checked from here; (f) rest is a treatment like the others and can harm; if you would rather it never did worse than the fever alone, the constraint is one line in `drawEffects` and the always-rest doctor then becomes a safe, mediocre strategy (about 160 of a thousand).

This game has had the builder's review only: screenshots and a scripted browser session at 380 px on two seeds and a laptop window. It has not been played by real strangers since the first build, and its length has not been measured.
