A province of France · 1690 to 1699

Leibniz’s
Register

You are the physician of a province. Every winter a fever comes, a hundred sick come to you with it, and you give them one remedy for the winter. Left to itself the fever kills about twenty of the hundred. The right remedy saves most of them; the wrong one kills more than the fever would; and the same remedy is right in one kind of winter and wrong in another. All that tells the kinds apart is two things: whether the winter is cold or mild, and whether it is dry or wet.

Nobody writes the winters down. In 1694 a letter from Mr Leibniz will change that, for every doctor in the kingdom.

Read the two cards of the winter. Read the letters, once there are any. Give the hundred sick one remedy.

Lose as few of the sick as you can in ten winters: each winter, give the one remedy that fits the kind of winter it is.

1694 · a letter arrives
From the First Doctor of the KingParis, July 1694

Mr Leibniz writes in the Journal des sçavans. In Modena a doctor has kept a history of each year, and in two years has already seen the bark succeed incomparably better in one than in the other. Winters like past winters come back; a doctor who has only his own winters to go on learns again from scratch, at the expense of the sick.

By order of the King, from this winter every physician in every province writes a few lines a year to me: the winter, what he gave the sick, what came of it. I send the whole collection back to each of you the following winter.

What one doctor sees in a lifetime, twelve see in a year.

The four winters behind you are yours alone. The first letters from the other provinces come back next winter.

The collection

How to play

Lose as few of the sick as you can in ten winters: each winter, give the one remedy that fits the kind of winter it is.

The winter

Two cards show the winter: cold (pale, snowflake) or mild (mustard, sun); dry (red, wind) or wet (teal, drops). That makes four kinds of winter, and the kind is all that matters.

The remedy

Three remedies: bark, bleeding, rest; rest is a treatment like the others, bed, warmth and broth by your order. Left to itself the fever kills about twenty of a hundred. In every kind of winter one remedy helps (five die), one is no better than the fever alone (twenty die), one harms (thirty die). Which is which depends only on the kind of winter, and the same remedy can help in one kind and harm in another. Tap a remedy to see the move, then tap Give it to the hundred sick. You see what the remedy you gave did; you never see what the others would have done.

What you have to go on

Your own past winters, every one, as small cards: the two cards of the winter, what you gave, what came of it. From 1695, the collection: every province's letters for every winter since 1694, grouped by kind of winter, with a count of how often each remedy helped, was no better than the fever alone, or harmed there, and your own winters among them. Tap it to open it and look for your winter's kind.

The end

After 1699 the game says what became of the sick, before the letter and after it, sets your ten winters beside three other doctors (memory alone, the letters from 1694, the letters from 1690, and one who knew every winter), and shows the ten winters as they were.
