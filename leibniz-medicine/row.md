| **Leibniz's Register** | Leibniz (1694), "On the Way of Perfecting Medicine", *Journal des sçavans*, 26 July 1694, pp. 338–340, in Lloyd Strickland's translation (2019). Years like past years come back, the same bark cures in one and fails in the next, and a doctor with no record learns from scratch at the expense of the sick; a register of a few lines a year would tell him. As a 1950s annual report in cream, charcoal, mustard, teal and tomato: you are the physician of a province from 1690 to 1700. Each year four season cards say what kind of year it is, a hundred Isotype figures are the sick who came, and you give them bark, bleeding or rest; the dead lie down in red. Your head holds last year whole, the year before in part, then nothing; in 1694 Leibniz's letter arrives and the register begins, every year written in ink and kept. The year that matches this one is sometimes last year, sometimes a page in the book, and twice a year nobody wrote down. At the end, your dead against three doctors on the same years: memory alone, the register from 1694, and the register Leibniz wished had been kept "for a while now". | [play](https://steffenhuck.github.io/paper-to-game/leibniz-medicine/leibnizs-register.html) · [memo](leibniz-medicine/memo.md) |
